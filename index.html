⛺ Camping weather checker

Should I go camping this weekend?

Check Friday, Saturday and Sunday with symbols for morning, afternoon, evening and overnight. Rain normally means CAUTION; thunder, lightning and dangerous gusts trigger WARNING.

The camping checker uses local hourly forecast data from Open-Meteo to assess rain risk, overnight wind gusts, temperature, storm signals and ground conditions. It gives a GO, CAUTION or WARNING for each day of the weekend so you can plan your arrival, pitching and overnight kit accordingly.

💧 Plant watering checker

Should I water my plants today?

Check recent rain, expected rain, temperature, wind, humidity and near-surface soil moisture signals.

The plant watering checker compares recent rainfall, forecast rain in the next 24 hours, temperature, wind speed, relative humidity and near-surface soil moisture data. It gives a WATER, CHECK SOIL or WAIT answer. Pots, hanging baskets and newly planted specimens dry out faster than established garden beds, so the checker always recommends checking those first even on a WAIT day.

🔥 BBQ weather checker

Should I BBQ today?

Check rain risk, wind, temperature, storm risk and the best outdoor cooking window.

The BBQ and outdoor grilling checker looks at rain probability, wind speed, gusts, storm signals and temperature to find the best cooking window between midday and 9 pm. Strong gusts make lighting and controlling a BBQ harder and less safe; thunder or lightning triggers a WARNING regardless of how dry the afternoon looks.
